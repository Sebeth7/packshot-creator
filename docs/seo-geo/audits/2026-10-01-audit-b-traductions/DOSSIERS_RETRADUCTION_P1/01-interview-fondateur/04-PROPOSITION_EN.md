# Laurent Wainberg, founder of PackshotCreator — Interview

Founded in 2001 [D33], Sysnext — the company behind the [**PackshotCreator**](/en/a-propos) concept — shook up the conventions of product photography. Its idea was simple but bold: make visual production faster, more accessible and more consistent for businesses. Ten years after it started, the company was present in more than 35 countries and had equipped nearly 8,000 businesses, with a conviction as strong as ever: **product visuals are a strategic driver of performance.**

## A long-misunderstood innovation

In its early days, PackshotCreator faced fierce criticism, particularly from some professional photographers. Automated LED light boxes were seen as a threat to the profession. “Some people declared photography dead. But the reality was quite different: the market had simply evolved, and we responded with the right solution,” recalls Laurent Wainberg.

E-commerce was booming, the number of SKUs to photograph kept growing and turnaround times were getting shorter. Businesses no longer needed just beautiful images: they needed **productivity**, **standardization** and **in-house control** over their visual production. That is exactly what PackshotCreator offered, long before automation became a buzzword.

## A business approach centered on use cases

PackshotCreator established itself as a full-fledged player in the digital commerce value chain, supporting not only online retailers but also marketing, R&D and communications departments across many industries. [Cosmetics](/en/industrie/cosmetiques-beaute), [manufacturing](/en/industrie/pieces-techniques-industrie), luxury goods, pharmaceuticals, [food](/en/industrie/food-alimentaire): use cases have multiplied, and so have features.

**Still photos, multi-angle views, 360° animations, hemispherical 3D views**… PackshotCreator studios have evolved as needs changed, while keeping the same philosophy: **making it easier to create high-quality, consistent and interactive visuals.**

![Demo space with several automated photo studios, including an Orbitvu Alphadesk shooting table](/images/blog/67e2aa0213158b42087cf052.avif)

### Sustained growth and international recognition

In 2011, Sysnext already had revenue of €4 million, up 11%. The following year got off to an even stronger start (+20%). This growth was built on a sustained R&D effort and a high-value customer support strategy.

Technology alone is not enough. What PackshotCreator understood very early on is that success also depends on **education and team onboarding**. “We have never settled for simply selling an automated photo studio. We have always focused on support, training and helping end users take ownership of the tool,” says Laurent Wainberg.

### From productivity to control of visual workflows

When deadlines are tight, content volumes are high and channels multiply, **automated photography is not just an option: it is a strategic response**. PackshotCreator lets businesses stay in control of their content production without sacrificing quality or creativity. By bringing these studios right into product or marketing teams, companies become more **agile, consistent and efficient**.

Photographers, for their part, have not been pushed out of the process. Quite the opposite: by taking repetitive, technical tasks off their hands, automated solutions let them **refocus on art direction, high-end retouching and high-value projects.**

### People at the heart of the approach

PackshotCreator also offers [**training on Orbitvu photo studios**](/fr/academy) (in French) for the teams who use them every day. It is further proof of the brand’s commitment to **sharing know-how and raising visual standards.**

## Conclusion

**PackshotCreator did not kill photography: it revealed new ways to use it.** With a vision firmly focused on business needs, the company succeeded in making automation a strategic ally for visual performance.

Today, [through its collaboration with Orbitvu](https://orbitvu.com/blog/packshotcreator-now-powered-orbitvu/) and solutions such as the [Alphashot Pro G2](/en/studio-photo/alphashot-pro-g2), PackshotCreator continues to innovate, with the same promise it has made from the start: helping brands create **striking, consistent, well-controlled images** on their own.

**Who invented the automated packshot concept?**

It was Laurent Wainberg, founder of Sysnext, who introduced the first automated product photography system in Europe, as early as 2003 [D33 : 2003 désigne ici le premier système, pas la création de Sysnext (2001) — à confirmer, voir 06, point 4]. The idea was simple but visionary: enable companies to produce quality visuals in-house, without always relying on an outside photo studio.

**Where does the name PackshotCreator come from?**

The name grew out of a desire to democratize the creation of product visuals, also known as packshots. With PackshotCreator, packshots were no longer reserved for agencies or specialist photographers: they became accessible, automated and repeatable in-house. The word “packshot” has since become standard across the industry. [À VALIDER : l'anglais actuel ajoute « a testament to the impact of the brand » ; lien de causalité sans source, retiré comme en FR — voir 06, point 10]

**How did the profession react to these innovations?**

At first, traditional photographers saw these systems as a threat. But over time, many realized they could rely on automation to save time on repetitive tasks and focus on high-value work: art direction, set and mood creation, visual storytelling…

**What was PackshotCreator’s contribution to the European market?**

PackshotCreator was the first player to structure the European market for automated photography. The company also played a strong educational role, explaining what was at stake in visual productivity, training many users and making the technology understandable for small and midsize businesses, large corporations and photographers themselves.

**Who is Orbitvu, and how does it fit into this story?**

Orbitvu is an innovative Polish company that emerged in the 2010s with a new generation of automated photo studios. In 2023, PackshotCreator became an official Orbitvu distributor in France and several French-speaking countries, adding these new solutions to its range: more efficient, more reliable and designed for today’s e-commerce and visual marketing standards.
