### Homogeneity and packshot photography: the secret to professional photos

#### Why is consistency essential for professional photos?

When starting to sell products online, a good presentation is essential to attract customers and convince them to buy, which is something we have seen and reviewed throughout this series. And homogeneity is part of a good presentation... In fact, it is even one of the most important principles in packshot photography when you have more than one product to present and sell. If the photos are not consistent with each other, this can quickly make you lose credibility and damage your brand image. Even worse, it can really **hinder the shopping experience and repel your users**. For example, imagine that you were looking to buy shoes online and that you came to a website presenting these products in this way:

This could discourage you and push you to look for products elsewhere, and that's normal. This is why visual consistency is important to create a pleasant user experience and to facilitate product comparison. To guarantee this homogeneity, it is therefore **It is important to ensure that every detail is the same from one photo shoot to the next**, details such as:

- lighting,

- the position of the product,

- camera settings,

- or even the background.

If any of these items differ from photo to photo, it can create a sense of confusion and inconsistency. As you can see, to sell products online effectively, it is essential to create a **smooth and quality user experience**. Having consistent photos for each product is a key element of this experience, making it easier to compare different products and create a strong and identifiable brand image.

#### How to ensure consistency between all your photos for a professional result?

First, make sure that **all photos are taken under the same conditions** lighting, focus, and background. That means you should use the same light type, focal length, settings, and background for each photo. If you're working with a professional photographer, make sure they know exactly what you expect and check their results during the photo shoot. Then, **use editing software to adjust** the contrast, color, and sharpness of each photo, while maintaining the natural appearance of the products photographed. This will allow you to**standardize all of your packshot photos** for a professional look. However, be careful not to retouch the images too much, at the risk of deceiving your customers.
