# Recover your PackshotCreator or Ortery software

Over the past 20-plus years, [PackshotCreator has helped thousands of companies produce professional-quality visuals in-house](/en/a-propos) with automated photography solutions. Since support ended, however, many users have contacted us with the same problem: lost PackshotCreator software, a studio **not recognized by Windows 11**, or no way to **reinstall the system** after changing computers.\
If this sounds familiar, here is what you need to know to understand the situation and find the right solution.

## Why is your software no longer available?

All the software supplied with PackshotCreator studios between 2003 and 2024 was developed by **Ortery Technologies**, a Taiwan-based company that has been a pioneer of automated photography since 2001.\
For more than 20 years, PackshotCreator was **Ortery's distributor** in the EMEA region and sold these software and hardware solutions under its own brand. Since the end of 2024, however:

- PackshotCreator **no longer distributes Ortery software**;
- **PackshotCreator support** for these solutions **officially ended on December 31, 2024**.

Software development continues at Ortery. For any technical question, reinstallation or license request, please **contact the publisher directly**.

## Windows 11 compatibility: significant limitations

Even if you find the software, there are some limitations to be aware of.\
Older versions of the Ortery software supplied with PackshotCreator studios have **limited compatibility with Windows 11**. Your **camera may no longer be recognized**, and installation may **fail or freeze** on recent systems.\
In these situations, getting the studio running again is not always possible: your hardware must still be supported, and your setup (camera, connections, operating system) must be compatible. If not, it is time to consider **migrating to a modern solution** that meets today's standards.

**Is Ortery software compatible with Windows 11?**

It depends on the version. The most recent Ortery software is compatible with Windows 11. However, the versions shipped with PackshotCreator studios between 2003 and 2020 generally are not. If you are still using an old version supplied by PackshotCreator, it will probably no longer work properly on current systems, due to driver incompatibilities, unrecognized cameras or general instability. To check your own setup, contact Ortery.

**Why not just keep using my PackshotCreator studio if Ortery is updating the software?**

Technically, you can try to get your studio running again by contacting Ortery directly. That assumes your hardware is still supported, your environment is compatible (camera, connections, operating system), and you are willing to do without the functional improvements offered by current solutions. In practice, keeping an old studio running often becomes a burden, in terms of both time and reliability.

**What is the point of migrating to Orbitvu if Ortery keeps developing its software?**

Orbitvu takes a more modern approach, designed for today's production workflows. Its studios include advanced features such as real-time background removal, virtual lighting and an intelligent assistant that guides the user. The entire process is centralized in a single program, Orbitvu Station, built for intensive professional use. Orbitvu also comes with full local support from PackshotCreator: demonstrations, training and integration advice.

**My Ortery/PackshotCreator studio still works. Why should I consider replacing it?**

A system that works is not necessarily an efficient one. The challenge is no longer keeping a studio running, but producing visuals quickly, consistently and without manual retouching. Orbitvu now enables autonomous production, consistent quality whoever the operator, and smooth integration with your business tools (PIM, e-commerce, DAM, etc.). Depending on the case, production time can be cut by a factor of three to five, with a direct impact on your costs and lead times.

**Do you offer upgrade or trade-in programs for old PackshotCreator or Ortery studios?**

Yes. PackshotCreator offers a trade-in program for customers with older PackshotCreator or Ortery studios. It includes a free assessment, a trade-in valuation of your equipment and a discount on a new Orbitvu studio suited to your needs (still packshots, 360° or video). We then support you throughout the project: demonstration, training and integration. The goal is a fast transition with no interruption to your business, so you can benefit from the latest technological advances.
