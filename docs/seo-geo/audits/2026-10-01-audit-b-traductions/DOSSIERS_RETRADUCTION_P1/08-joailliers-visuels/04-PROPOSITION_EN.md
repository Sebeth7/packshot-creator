# Corps

[Photographing jewelry](/en/industrie/bijoux-joaillerie) takes **rare precision** to capture all of its **beauty** and **sparkle**. Diamonds, rubies, sapphires, or emeralds: every precious stone has its own story and deserves a tailored approach that fully reveals its natural splendor. Here is how to photograph your jewelry and turn it into striking visuals, using modern, effective techniques.

## Jewelry photography: a demanding art

Jewelry photography calls for far closer attention to **detail**, **light**, and **focus** than photographing bulky objects. Every piece has its own distinctive features: the **brilliance of the stones**, the **delicacy of the setting**, or the **texture of the materials used**.

## Choosing a specialized photo studio: essential technical criteria

Choosing the right studio is critical to meeting the technical demands of jewelry photography. Studios designed for macro photography and small objects, such as the [Orbitvu Alphashot Micro V2](/en/studio-photo/alphashot-micro-v2), offer several decisive advantages. They reproduce nuances and hues exactly with **accurate color rendering**, keep the entire depth of the piece uniformly sharp through [**focus stacking**](/en/guide/how-to-do-focus-stacking-for-bracelet-photography), and provide **precise, adjustable lighting** that gives you fine control over reflections and sparkle.

## Advanced lighting techniques to enhance precious stones

To fully reveal the brilliance of a diamond, a ruby, or an emerald, mastering **lighting** is essential. With adjustable light sources, you can bring out the key characteristics of the stones: their **cut, clarity, polish, and symmetry**. Well-controlled lighting faithfully renders the brilliance found in high-end jewelry and eliminates unwanted shadows that could hide important details.

![Gold-colored ring set with an emerald-cut pink stone, photographed from the front on a light background](/images/blog/67dbae80db22afe6492e9626.avif)

## Practical tips for better jewelry photography

A few simple tips can noticeably improve the quality of your photos. Choose a neutral background, white or light gray: it sets off the piece and simplifies cutouts in post-production. Adjust your lighting carefully, starting at low intensity and increasing it gradually to control reflections. Use light diffusers to avoid overly strong reflections on shiny surfaces. Finally, for a 360° animation, rotate the piece rather than the camera: the result will be more consistent.

# FAQ

[Q1] **How does high-end jewelry photography differ from other types of product photography?**

High-end jewelry photography goes well beyond simple visual documentation: it is precision work on an almost microscopic scale. Unlike standard product photography, it demands advanced technical skill and an in-depth understanding of the optical properties of gemstones. Every piece of jewelry is unique, and each stone has its own refraction, light dispersion, and crystal structure. Beyond photographic expertise, it therefore takes gemological knowledge to faithfully capture the character and true value of each creation.

[Q2] **How many photos can I expect to take per day with the right equipment?**

According to the Gad & Co testimonial quoted in this article, specialized equipment such as the Orbitvu Alphashot Micro makes it possible to take more than 100 photos per day while maintaining consistent quality.

[Q3] **What technical criteria are essential when choosing a photo studio for jewelry?**

A suitable studio should provide accurate color rendering that reproduces nuances and hues exactly, uniform sharpness across the entire depth of the piece through focus stacking, and precise, adjustable lighting for fine control over reflections and sparkle.

[Q4] **How does Hyperfocus technology improve sharpness in jewelry images?**

Hyperfocus technology, or advanced focus stacking, is a significant development for fine jewelry photography. Unlike traditional focus stacking, it relies on proprietary algorithms that analyze the piece's three-dimensional relief and automatically calculate the most suitable focus sequence. The Alphashot Micro V2 can capture up to 200 images at different focal planes, in micrometric steps as fine as 10 microns, then merge them while preserving depth information. [À VALIDER : mêmes chiffres non sourcés qu'en FR ; voir 06, point 3.] This technique works around the shallow depth of field of macro lenses: the finest details of the setting, the surface texture of the metal, and the internal characteristics of the stones all appear sharp in a single image, a level of detail that is hard to achieve with conventional methods.

[Q5] **How do you create professional-quality 360° animations?**

Rotate the piece rather than the camera for a consistent result, keep the lighting constant throughout the sequence, and use specialized equipment such as the Alphashot Micro, which automates the process. Finally, make sure the animations are smooth and optimized for e-commerce platforms.
